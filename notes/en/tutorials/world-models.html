About this English page

The tutorial itself is written in Chinese. This page is its English companion: it gives the complete chapter-by-chapter structure and states what each chapter argues. Every derivation, table, diagram and per-paper breakdown lives in the Chinese edition; the paper library is fully browsable in English.

What this tutorial is about ​

A world model in the video-generation sense is a simulator you can act inside: give it a state and an action, and it returns the next observation. The tutorial argues that video is the natural universal interface for this, because it is the one modality with internet-scale pretraining data and a shared representation across games, driving, indoor scenes and robots.

It then separates two things that are frequently conflated. Action-conditioned world models are judged on whether the generated future obeys the action that was supplied. Exploratory world models are judged on whether a place you revisit still looks like the place you left — memory consistency, not action following. Section 1.5 shows how the two relate; Chapter 7.2 names the resulting central tension, responsiveness versus consistency, and Chapter 7.3 proposes the persistent state core as the direction that might resolve it.
