Acceleration & Distillation in Video Generation ​

Full text (Chinese): 视频生成的加速与蒸馏Coverage: June 2025 – July 2026, with earlier foundational work included as background. Papers: 59 deep-read notes

About this English page

The tutorial itself is written in Chinese. This page is its English companion: it gives the complete chapter-by-chapter structure and states what each chapter argues, so you can navigate the Chinese text, cite it, or decide whether it is worth reading. Every derivation, table, diagram and per-paper breakdown lives in the Chinese edition; the paper library is fully browsable in English.

What this tutorial is about ​

Video diffusion is slow for three separable reasons, and almost every acceleration paper attacks exactly one of them: the number of denoising steps Nstep​, the cost of a single forward pass Cmodel​, and the number of spatio-temporal tokens Ltoken​. The tutorial builds that decomposition first, then uses it as the coordinate system for everything else — so that a reader can look at any new method and immediately say which term it reduces and what it trades away.

Its central technical claim is that step distillation splits cleanly into trajectory distillation (make the student follow the teacher's ODE path) and distribution distillation (make the student's output distribution match the teacher's), that adversarial objectives are a third axis rather than a competitor, and that the defining trend of 2026 is hybridisation of all three.
