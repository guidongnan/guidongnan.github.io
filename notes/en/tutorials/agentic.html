Agentic Video Generation ​

Full text (Chinese): 智能体视频生成Coverage: June 2025 – July 2026, tracing foundational work back to 2023. Papers: 29 deep-read notes

About this English page

The tutorial itself is written in Chinese. This page is its English companion: it gives the complete chapter-by-chapter structure and states what each chapter argues. Every derivation, table, diagram and per-paper breakdown lives in the Chinese edition; the paper library is fully browsable in English.

What this tutorial is about ​

The shift the tutorial describes is from "an artist producing a finished drawing in one pass" to "a film crew working together". A single forward pass through a video model cannot hold a multi-shot story consistent, cannot check its own output, and cannot revise. Agentic video generation replaces that single pass with a loop: plan · generate · evaluate · revise.

Two technical families are separated throughout. Family A is orchestration — an external agent (or several) decomposes the task, calls generators and tools, keeps state, and verifies results. Family B is reasoning-native generation, where the reasoning is internal to the model. The tutorial also defines a five-level ladder of "how agentic" a system is, from prompt rewriting up to L5 reasoning-native generation, and uses it to place every system it discusses.
