About this English page

The tutorial itself is written in Chinese. This page is its English companion: it gives the complete chapter-by-chapter structure and states what each chapter argues. Every derivation, table, diagram and per-paper breakdown lives in the Chinese edition; the paper library is fully browsable in English.

What this tutorial is about ​

Video generation can serve robotics in five distinct roles, and the tutorial's contribution is refusing to blur them: (1) generation as planning or policy, (2) prediction as representation, (3) world model / simulator / evaluator, (4) data engine, and (5) unified VLA plus world model. A paper that is excellent in role 4 may be worthless in role 1, and most confusion in the literature comes from comparing systems across roles.

Running through every chapter is what the tutorial calls the soul question: do you actually have to generate pixels? Chapter 7.1 makes the case on both sides — pixels give a general, pretrainable interface and a human-inspectable artefact, but they cost latency, and much of the information they carry is irrelevant to control. Chapter 6 answers the companion question of evaluation with a five-level ladder that ends at closed-loop task success, and adds a fifth level for the credibility of world models used as evaluators.
