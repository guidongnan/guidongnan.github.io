About this English page

The tutorial itself is written in Chinese. This page is its English companion: it gives the complete chapter-by-chapter structure and states what each chapter argues. Every derivation, table, diagram and per-paper breakdown lives in the Chinese edition; the paper library is fully browsable in English.

What this tutorial is about ​

This is the panoramic entry point of the collection: the perception–cognition–action loop, what makes embodiment fundamentally harder than pure vision or language, and how the field's methods, data and benchmarks fit together.

Four gaps separate embodied AI from NLP and CV, and the tutorial keeps returning to them: the agent's own actions change its data distribution; supervision is expensive because it comes from real hardware; success is defined by task completion rather than by a differentiable loss; and evaluation cannot be reproduced from a static test set. It then offers a single "capability dimensions" framework for judging whether an embodied system is any good, and a stack diagram that places every method family discussed later.
