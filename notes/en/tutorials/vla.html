Vision-Language-Action Models (VLA) ​

Full text (Chinese): 视觉-语言-动作模型 VLACoverage: June 2025 – August 2026, tracing back to RT-1/RT-2, Open X-Embodiment, OpenVLA, π0, FAST and Diffusion Policy. Scale: 11 chapters + 2 appendices; Chapter 11 dissects 264 papers by arXiv id.

About this English page

The tutorial itself is written in Chinese. This page is its English companion: it gives the complete chapter-by-chapter structure and states what each chapter argues. Every derivation, table, diagram and per-paper breakdown lives in the Chinese edition; the paper library is fully browsable in English.

What this tutorial is about ​

The tutorial assumes you know what a Transformer and a diffusion model are, and assumes nothing about robotics. It starts from a language-conditioned POMDP and the compounding-error bound of behaviour cloning, and derives from there why the field looks the way it does.

Its stated discipline about evidence is worth repeating, because it shapes the whole text: every number is attributed to the paper it came from, and wherever the author is offering an opinion rather than a fact, the text says so. Chapter 8 is described as "the dirtiest part of the field" and Chapter 8.6 argues that statistical methodology is the thing embodied AI is most short of.

Three claims carry the argument:

  • Action representation is the most underrated design decision in VLA (§2.3). It determines what the action head can express, how the backbone must be coupled, and what the latency budget looks like.
  • Latency is not the same as model speed (§2.9). A fast model inside a slow loop is a slow robot, and the maths of that gap is developed explicitly.
  • The architecture space is ten families, not two (Chapter 3): monolithic discrete, VLM + action expert, dual-system/hierarchical, 3D/spatial, latent action, world-action, reasoning, memory, multi-sensory, and cross-embodiment.
