About

I am a Ph.D. student in the USTC–MSRA joint Ph.D. program, based at the University of Science and Technology of China and doing my research at Microsoft Research Asia. I work on video generation and world models.

What attracts me is building something that is visually fancy and has never existed before. In generative video that is not a matter of tuning numbers, but sometimes the capability is missing outright, and the work is to invent the mechanism that makes it possible to produce something new. What I am after is using algorithms to generate visual experience that is richer and stranger than what past methods can achieve.
