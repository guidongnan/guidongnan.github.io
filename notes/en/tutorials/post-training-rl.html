Post-Training & Reinforcement Learning in Video Generation ​

Full text (Chinese): 视频生成中的后训练与强化学习Coverage: June 2025 onward, with the foundational 2023 – mid-2025 work read paper by paper. Papers: 61 deep-read notes

About this English page

The tutorial itself is written in Chinese. This page is its English companion: it gives the complete chapter-by-chapter structure and states what each chapter argues. Every derivation, table, diagram and per-paper breakdown lives in the Chinese edition; the paper library is fully browsable in English.

What this tutorial is about ​

The framing analogy is explicit: pretraining → post-training in video generation plays the same role as GPT → ChatGPT in language. Pretraining buys competence; post-training buys usefulness and alignment, and there is a specific class of problems — aesthetic preference, motion quality, instruction compliance, physical plausibility — that more pretraining data does not fix.

The technical key is Chapter 2's reframing: one denoising or integration trajectory is a decision trajectory, so sampling is an MDP. Once that is accepted, three families follow — reward backpropagation, direct preference optimisation (the DPO family), and policy gradients with GRPO — and the tutorial gives a decision tree for choosing among them plus the three adversaries that stalk all three: reward hacking, distribution collapse, and the cost of video rollouts.
