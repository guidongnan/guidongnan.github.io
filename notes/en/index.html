Tutorial map ​

The thirteen tutorials chain together along a single thread — video generation → world models → embodied AI — with video understanding supplying the perception and evaluation groundwork. Use the diagram below to pick a reading path.

How to use this collection ​

  • New to the field: start with Autoregressive Video Generation to build paradigm intuition, then branch into physics awareness, acceleration, or agentic generation as your interests dictate.
  • Working on world models / embodied AI: World Models → World-Action Models (WAM) → Video Generation for Embodied AI → General Embodied AI closes the loop from "simulator" to "deployment".
  • Already doing video generation, want to move to WAM: read From Video Generation to WAM — it views WAM from outside video generation. Chapter 3's five discontinuities tell you which habits have to change; Chapter 11 dissects 206 papers one at a time.
  • Want to build a WAM directly: jump to Chapter 7 of World-Action Models, "Building a working WAM from scratch" — a complete build guide from choosing a base model to closing the loop on real hardware.
  • Working on robot policies: read Vision-Language-Action Models. Chapter 2 lays out the full design space of action representation and backbone coupling, Chapter 8 lists the places evaluation is most easily gamed, and Chapter 11 dissects 264 papers by arXiv id.
  • Looking something up: the 🔍 button in the top bar searches the whole site; every tutorial carries its own table of contents and paper list with arXiv ids.
  • New to video understanding: start with Video Understanding Basics. Chapter 2 covers the principles, Chapter 4 is a dedicated chapter on evaluation methodology, and Chapter 10 explains all 394 cited papers one by one.
  • Working on long video: read Long Video Understanding. Chapter 1 explains why the obvious approach fails, Chapter 5 is a dedicated chapter on evaluation shortcuts, Chapter 7 gives a selection decision tree over ten method families, and Chapters 11–12 dissect all 1546 cited papers.

About this English edition ​

The tutorials were written in Chinese. This English edition currently provides, for each of the thirteen tutorials, a full English chapter-by-chapter outline with a summary of what each chapter argues, plus a link to the complete Chinese text.

The paper library is fully usable in English: all 1,133 entries are listed under their original English arXiv titles, grouped by tutorial, with submission dates and arXiv subject categories. Each entry links both to the original paper and to its Chinese deep-read note.
